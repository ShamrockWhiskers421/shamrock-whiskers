About the club

A new home for TICA enthusiasts in Ireland.

Shamrock Whiskers was created to support and grow TICA showing in Ireland, while keeping the atmosphere friendly, inclusive and enjoyable for newcomers and experienced exhibitors alike.

Whether you show pedigreed cats, household pets, breed, judge, volunteer, or simply love cats, we would be delighted to have you involved.
