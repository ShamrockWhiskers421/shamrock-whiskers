01 / OUR STORY
☘

For the love of cats.
And the people who adore them.

Shamrock Whiskers is a welcoming Irish cat club celebrating the beauty, character and companionship of cats. We're building a community where experienced exhibitors, new cat owners and curious visitors can come together.

Through our shows and club activities, we hope to share knowledge, make lasting friendships and celebrate cats of every kind.
